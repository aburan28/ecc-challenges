The challenge

ECC2K-130

The current campaign uses the established Frobenius sigma walk at distinguished-point weight 32. Workers receive a unique seed namespace and keep checkpoints locally so interrupted work can resume.

Verification is part of the computation.

A syntactically valid point does not prove a worker followed the walk. Submitted batches stay separate from the existing campaign table; selected trails and any collision lead require independent replay.

Raw objects, digests, audit outcomes, and queue offsets are retained so the process can be checked later.
