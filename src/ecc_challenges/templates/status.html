Reading the numbers

Progress needs evidence.

An archived distinguished point is an accepted upload. The gateway checks selected trails independently and indexes eligible records. Point counts alone do not give a reliable fraction of the work needed to find a collision.

A completion chance will appear only after the campaign has a verified operation count and a documented collision model. Historical points in the earlier account are not included here until an audited import is complete.
