Reading the names. ECC2K is a Koblitz curve, also called an anomalous binary curve; ECC2 is a randomly chosen curve over a binary field GF(2m); ECCp is a randomly chosen curve over a prime field GF(p). The number is roughly the size in bits of the group being attacked, so challenges are grouped by field size: ECC2K-130, ECC2-131 and ECCp-131 are the three 131-bit curves. Koblitz curves are the easiest of each size, because the Frobenius map lets rho skip work.
