([PTX A/B](l4-run-1/receipt.json), [native A/B](native-ab/receipt.json),

read as positive DP emission or long-run DP-density evidence.

used the frozen positive DP1 cases and checked nonempty outputs across all 24
curves. Its nine requested binary curves had 137 expected and GPU-matched
